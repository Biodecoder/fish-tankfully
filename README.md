# Fish Tankfully (`缸好有鱼`) — v0.1

A self-contained 3D web recreation of **Fish Tankfully (`缸好有鱼`)** — a cozy desktop idle aquarium, aquascaping DIY editor, realistic water-chemistry ecosystem simulator, DSLR macro photography studio, and wild stream dip-netting (`野采`) game built with Three.js and WebAudio.

## Quick Start

```bash
npm install
npm start
# Open http://127.0.0.1:8095/
```

## Features in v0.1

- **Cozy Sunlit Office Desk & 4 Switchable Aquariums**:
  - *Grand Nature Biotope (`大鱼缸`, 120cm)*, *Mecha Ruins Diorama (`机甲生态缸`, 90cm)*, *Nano Cube (`小鱼缸`, 45cm)*, and *Cozy Desktop Bowl (`圆缸`, 30cm)*.
  - Framed by a sunlit window with golden-hour sunbeams, a sleeping low-poly calico cat, a steaming ceramic coffee mug, a blue cassette Walkman, wall posters (`BACK TO WORK`, `PROFITS`), a 65% mechanical keyboard, and a spinning vinyl turntable.
- **16 Faceted 3D Ornamental Fish & Shrimp Species**:
  - Neon Tetra (`红绿灯`), Cardinal Tetra (`宝莲灯`), White Cloud Mountain Minnow (`白云金丝`), Pale Chub (`宽鳍鱲`), Rosy Barb (`玫瑰鲃`), Tiger Barb (`虎皮鱼`), High-Fin Bitterling (`高帆鳑鲏`), Altum Angelfish (`埃及神仙`), Golden Angelfish (`黄金神仙`), German Blue Ram (`荷兰凤凰`), Sky Blue Guppy (`天空蓝孔雀`), Full Red Albino Guppy (`全红孔雀`), Panda Cory (`熊猫鼠`), Rainbow Stiphodon Goby (`周氏吻虾虎`), Red Cherry Shrimp (`极火虾`), and Red Crystal Shrimp (`红白水晶虾`).
  - Supports **Fry (`幼年`) → Adult (`成年`)** growth, **`【鱼王】` (Fish King)** crowns, and **`【闪光】` (Shiny)** color morphs.
- **23+ Aquascaping Hardscape, Plants, Tools & Equipment**:
  - Includes the signature **Overgrown Mecha Head Relic (`残破机甲头雕`)**, Pine Bonsai Driftwood (`迎客松沉木`), Seiryu Monolith Peak (`青龙石主峰`), Floating Lotus Pads (`睡莲浮叶`), Scarlet Rotala (`红宫廷`), Canister Filter (`外置过滤桶`), and Overhead Studio LED Lamps (`W水族灯`).
- **Interactive Tank Care & DIY Aquascaping (`随心造景`)**:
  - `[1]` Magnetic Glass Algae Scraper (`鱼缸擦`) to scrub green algae off the front glass in real time.
  - `[2]` Sinking Pellets (`普通沉粮`) & `[3]` Golden Color-Enhancing Food (`增色鱼粮`).
  - `[4]` Methylene Blue (`亚甲基蓝`) water stabilizer & Siphon Water Change (`换水`).
  - `[B]` 3D DIY Aquascaping Grid with drag-and-drop placement, elevation/scale sliders, and $45^\circ$ rotation.
- **Coco's Aquarium Shop (`水族店`) & Tank Roster (`鱼缸信息`)**:
  - Daily Deals (`今日特惠`), Live Fish (`水中鱼`), and Tank Supplies (`缸中物`), plus full fish inspection and selling.
- **DSLR Macro Camera Viewfinder (`微距摄影`)**:
  - Adjustable aperture (`f/1.4–f/22`), focal length (`24mm–300mm`), and `ISO 100–1600`.
- **Wild Foraging Map (`野采地图`) & 3D Stream Dip-Netting (`溪流野采`)**:
  - Explore 4 regional waterways (*Shallows*, *River Bend*, *Longkou Old Town Creek*, *Canyon Deep Pool*), scoop wild fish with a 3D dip-net, and inspect your catch in a first-person low-poly hand reveal.
